Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used. Last updated September 20, 2026.

How this site works

This is a static showcase website for original paintings. There are no user accounts, no shopping cart, and no checkout. To buy or ask about a painting, you contact the gallery directly via WhatsApp or email.

Information you share with us

When you send an enquiry — by WhatsApp to +509 3424-9786 or by email to levoyexilgallery@gmail.com — you share your contact details and your message so the gallery can respond. That information is used only to handle your enquiry and any sale that follows from it.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (for example, the pages visited and an approximate location derived from your IP address) to keep the site secure and running reliably. This site does not use advertising trackers.

What this site never collects

Because there is no checkout, this website never collects payment or card details, and it never takes website orders.

How your information is used

To respond to your enquiries; to arrange viewings, sales, and shipping you ask about; and to keep the website secure.

Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Details you share are only passed on where needed to complete something you asked for — for example, a shipping carrier when arranging delivery — or where required by law.

Your rights

You may ask what information the gallery holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to levoyexilgallery@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
