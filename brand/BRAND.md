## Official brand blue — #0e3c79

**Locked 2026-09-20 by Daphnee.** This is the one and only official brand blue.

- Verified against the original website (2026-09-20): its `<meta name="theme-color">` is
  `#0e3c79`, and the blue sampled from the original site's actual favicon file
  (`cropped-Levoy-Exil-ICON-color-469-X-469-192x192.png`) is `#0e3c79`.
- The `#283d75` that appeared in earlier notes was an artifact of sampling a
  screenshot — it is **retired** and must not be used as a brand color.
- The new site already uses `#0e3c79` site-wide (header, footer, buttons, accents),
  so no recolor was needed.
