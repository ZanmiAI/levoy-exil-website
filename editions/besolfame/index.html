About this edition

This is a fine-art print adapted from Besolfame, the original 12 x 16 inch acrylic on canvas by Levoy Exil — a Levoy Exil design for collectors, not the original painting. The original remains available through the gallery.

The edition is strictly limited to 50 prints. Every buyer receives a numbered digital Certificate of Authenticity (No. X of 50) by email after purchase. The print itself is unsigned and unnumbered — your edition number lives on the certificate.

Once all 50 prints are claimed, the edition is permanently retired and never reprinted.
